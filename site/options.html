Options trading basics

An option is a contract on 100 shares of a stock.

Key terms

  • Call: right to buy at the strike price. Put: right to sell.
  • Strike: the agreed price. Expiration: the last day the contract is valid.
  • Premium: the option's price per share (x100 per contract).
  • In / at / out of the money: strike below, near or above the stock price (for calls).
  • Delta: sensitivity to the stock price, and a rough chance of finishing in the money.

How to place an options trade

  1. Apply for options approval with your broker.
  2. Open the option chain for a ticker.
  3. Pick expiration, then strike.
  4. Choose the action: Buy to Open, Sell to Open, Buy to Close or Sell to Close.
  5. Use a limit order near the mid price between bid and ask.

Risks to respect

  • Bought options can expire worthless: you can lose 100% of the premium.
  • Time decay (theta) works against buyers and for sellers.
  • Selling uncovered options can mean large losses. Beginners should stay with defined-risk or covered strategies.
  • Wide bid/ask spreads on thin options quietly cost money.
